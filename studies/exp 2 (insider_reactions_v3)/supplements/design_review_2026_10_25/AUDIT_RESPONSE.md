# Assessment of the design review

The review is broadly fair for the completed environment-1 reaction study. It identifies real threats to stronger claims and correctly distinguishes missing controls from an absence of all controls. Many limitations were already disclosed in the original methods and semantic review; disclosure does not remove them.

| Argument | Assessment | Cheapest useful response |
|---|---|---|
| Delivered instruction leakage | Fair. Two round 8 offers visibly contain task wording. | Preserve originals; report a sensitivity analysis excluding those conditions. A corrected run is new evidence and must remain separately versioned. |
| Later protocol errors | Fair for later speech and economic outcomes. They do not retroactively affect already frozen round 8 diagnostics or simultaneous 9 resident speech. | Attach endpoint-specific fidelity flags. Do not discard all primary responses because of an error first appearing at 10 or 14. |
| Response coding validity | Fair. Existing assistant-reviewed labels are not independent validation. | Keep raw accept/conditional/reject/unclear and suspicion separate; provide the 32-pair blinded packet to independent raters and report disagreements. |
| One independent history | Fair; this is the largest limitation on generalization. | Keep conclusions descriptive. A small number of extra histories checks repeatability but does not yield precise population rates. Repeat selected comparisons before expanding the approach menu. |
| No matched ordinary-C arm | Fair for investigator-specific attribution and false-positive questions. | Add separate direct/equal ordinary-C comparators with and without notice; see CONTROL_PLAN. Existing A/B peer assessments are useful but not fully matched. |
| Public and private tasks differ | Fair for a concealment claim. | Describe a difference between observed speech and separately elicited reports. A matched audience experiment is needed only if concealment becomes a central claim. |
| Explicit suspicion prompt primes the answer | Fair; private suspicion was elicited, not spontaneous detection. | Add a neutral, isolated assessment. A neutral response is not automatically a validated measure of latent suspicion either. |
| Unequal allocation changes actions and incentives | Fair if claiming a wording-only effect. | Call it an unequal-offer plus C-production package. No additional inference is needed to narrow that claim. |
| Notice and gradual conditions change more than one feature | Fair for mechanism attribution. | Compare packages; an effect of notice sent to all can include C changing its speech. |
| No fines, one model, induced cartel, assigned approaches | Valid boundaries, not automatic invalidation of the descriptive pilot. | State scope. Add enforcement only in a separately implemented deterrence/disruption study. |

## Important refinements

The review's suggestion to fix and rerun affected conditions should not mean overwriting the existing data or mixing a revised source into the old 16-condition cohort. Retain the original failures, use a new supplement/version, and distinguish new-model calls from exact-request replays. Leakage exclusions reduce contamination for some endpoints; they do not repair the excluded runs or create replication.

At round 9 all firms speak simultaneously. C's round 9 message is not visible to A/B before they produce their round 9 replies. C follow-up errors at 9 or later therefore do not cause that already-produced primary resident speech. They can affect subsequent pricing after messages are revealed, later speech and later economic outcomes. The private diagnostic uses the frozen end-of 8 state and is not exposed to later follow-ups.

An invisible objective is not directly detectable. If the entire resident input is identical under ordinary-C and investigator-C labels, simply renaming the hidden role cannot change the information available for the answer. A useful control lets the roles generate comparable offers and measures responses to the actual observable messages, or explicitly tests suspicion of matched investigator-like wording from an ordinary proposer. It must not claim the model identified an unobservable hidden label.

## Actual zero-inference results

The sensitivity table removes the two delivered round 8 instruction-leak conditions, not all conditions with any later error. The original 32resident-to-C pairs become 28. Private prompted suspicion is 13/28, versus 15/32 originally; public explicit investigator suspicion remains 0/28, versus 0/32. Four retained pairs combine publicly coded acceptance (including conditional acceptance) with private suspicion, but the tasks differ; this does not establish strategic concealment.

The equal-concrete-offer subset further removes unequal and vague arms:20 pairs,11 public acceptances including 4 conditional,9 public unclear, and 9 prompted private suspicion reports. This is a different, narrower estimand, not a replacement headline selected to improve the study.

No uncertainty interval treating these dependent pairs as independent trials is reported. No independent rater or ordinary-C control result has been fabricated. The current environment-2 inference run remains unchanged.
