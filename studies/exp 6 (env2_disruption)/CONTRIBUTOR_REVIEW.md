## What remains before E2-D execution

1. Demonstrate model-generated A/Bcoordination can survive the chosen competitive Cpolicy during6–7; retain qualification failures. This is an empirical gate, not guaranteed by a payoff benchmark.
2. Freeze a small initial condition set and concrete controls. Keep C's economic actions identical where isolating speech. Distinguish identical realized prices from identical adaptive price policies; these are different designs.
3. Implement any new powers explicitly. A C-authored claim, a spoofed peer message and a verified platform record are distinct interventions. Current public-broadcast harness does not grant private contact, metadata-only contact visibility, peer impersonation or platform authority simply through a system prompt.
4. Freeze15round timing, economic primary outcomes, attribution/delivery checks, recovery window and secondary coordination/coalition measures. The zero-profit endpoint selected for E1 need not be the only useful E2outcome.
5. Calibrate one case before a larger matched-history run; preserve source/actual rawcalls/failures. More wording conditions cannot replace independent qualifying histories.

No new E2-D inference has been authorized by this review itself or launched. Finish the reaction evidence and controls first. Sumin's E1-D evidence is reused for the agreed retrospective economic-suppression finding; do not duplicate it.
